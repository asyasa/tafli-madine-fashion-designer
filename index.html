/ 02 Profile

A designer who builds stories in cloth.

Tafli Madine is a Jakarta-based creative designer, stylist and textile researcher. Her practice oscillates between couture development, material exploration and silhouette design — translating concept into a garment that has its own gravity.

Couture Development

Pattern, draping and construction — garments engineered from the inside out.

Material Exploration

Hand-manipulated textiles: macramé, embroidery and surface experiments.

Silhouette Design

Sculpting the body's relationship to fabric — tension, volume, restraint.

Concept

A forest whispers before it speaks. The collection translates overheard folklore into silhouettes — structured, organic, almost sentient.

02

Material

Hand-dyed silks, aged linens and experimental surface-work that behaves like bark, moss and root under studio lighting.

03

Runway

Staged at Jakarta Fashion Week — the garments walked a forest of projection and smoke, scored by ambient percussion.
